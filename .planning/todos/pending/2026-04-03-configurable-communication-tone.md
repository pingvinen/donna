## Problem

Donna's text output currently has a fixed communication style. Users may want to customize the tone — playful, professional, terse, etc. — to make the experience more personal and enjoyable.

## Solution

Add a configurable "speech module" or tone setting that adjusts how Donna frames its output text (banners, summaries, status messages). Could be a setting in Donna's config that selects from predefined tone profiles or accepts a custom tone description.
